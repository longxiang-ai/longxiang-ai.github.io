01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity, in every dimension.

Mingwei Li / 李明伟↗

Ph.D. Student · AI Researcher

I build models that understand
and create the visual world.

I am a third-year Ph.D. student at the College of Artificial Intelligence, Zhejiang University in Hangzhou, China, advised by Prof. Yi Yang. My research centers on generative visual intelligence, spanning controllable image and video generation, 3D vision, transparent-object geometry estimation, and digital humans. As part of my doctoral training, I am jointly trained with Beijing Zhongguancun Academy under the mentorship of Prof. Tie-Yan Liu.

I am also a research intern at ByteDance, working on video generation and editing. Prior to that, I obtained my B.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from Zhejiang University in 2023 (Outstanding Graduate, ranked 2nd in major). I was also a member of the Mixed Class at Chu Kochen Honors College (CKC) of Zhejiang University.
