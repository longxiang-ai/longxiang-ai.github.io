### 🔬 Research Interests

My research focuses on **3D Computer Vision** and **Deep Learning**, with specific interests in:

- **3D/4D Reconstruction**: Multi-view stereo, neural radiance fields (NeRF), and 3D Gaussian Splatting
- **Surface Normal Estimation**: Foundation models for normal estimation of **transparent and reflective objects**
- **Generative Models**: Diffusion models for 3D content generation
